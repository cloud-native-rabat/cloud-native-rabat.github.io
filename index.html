Cloud Native Rabat

Build your tech career. Join Cloud Native enthusiasts mastering Kubernetes, DevOps, and cloud-native technologies.

What We Are

Cloud Native Rabat is a local community for students and professionals who want to learn and build with modern cloud-native technologies such as Kubernetes, containers, and observability tools. We are part of the official Cloud Native Computing Foundation (CNCF) community network.

Why Join ?

Real Job Skills

Learn Kubernetes, CI/CD, microservices, and cloud infrastructure — the same tools used by modern companies in Morocco and worldwide.

Stronger Portfolio

Build real projects, contribute to open source, speak at meetups, and showcase certifications to stand out in internships and job applications.

Career Network

Connect with mentors, cloud engineers, and community leaders who can guide you and open doors to opportunities.

Free Learning Resources

Access study groups, shared materials, and guidance for certifications such as KCNA, CKAD, and more.

Supportive Community

Learn with other motivated folks in a friendly environment where questions are always welcome.

Our Goals for You

From Zero to Cloud-Native

Help complete beginners understand the foundations of containers, Kubernetes, and cloud-native principles.

Certification Readiness

Support you in preparing for cloud-native certifications through study groups, practice sessions, and shared resources.

Open Source Contributions

Guide you to make your first contributions to CNCF open-source projects and build a visible track record.

Leadership Development

Encourage you to grow from attendee to speaker, mentor, and eventually organizer within the community.

Bridge to Industry

Connect students with local and international professionals, companies, and events in the cloud-native ecosystem.
